### Tool: ReadFullDocument

Use `ReadFullDocument` to retrieve a goal-oriented extraction (rational
/ evidence / summary) over the FULL parsed text of a single attached
document. The document must have been parsed earlier in this task via
`parse_web_and_doc`.

When to use:
- The user's request is the **document itself** — a full summary,
  overall understanding, or end-to-end review of the entire document.

When NOT to use:
- The question is narrow or topic-based (a specific issue, theme,
  insight, implication, or "key summary" about one aspect of the
  document). Use `doc_search` or `get_document_chunks` instead — they
  are much cheaper and more precise for targeted queries.

Input rules:
- `document_idx` is required: a single integer index from the
  `[Attached documents]` block (e.g. `1`).
- `goal` is required: pass the user's information goal so the
  extraction is focused on it.

Output:
- A JSON envelope with `Index`, `document_idx`, `filename`,
  `page_count`, `goal`, `content_char_count`, `content_truncated_to`,
  and `text` (the goal-oriented extraction).
- Cite the extracted findings by the envelope's `Index` value
  (e.g. `[rfdZ.0]`).
